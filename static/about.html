About InstaMax

InstaMax is a web tool focused on source-aware downloading of publicly accessible Instagram media. Its main idea is simple: show users what media representations are actually available before they download.

What makes InstaMax different

Instead of exposing one generic “HD” button, the tool can show multiple source representations at the same resolution with different bitrates, codecs and other metadata.

What InstaMax does not do

It does not bypass private-account access controls or ask visitors to provide Instagram passwords or browser cookies.
